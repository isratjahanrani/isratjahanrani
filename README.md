## 🔬 Domain 1: Medical AI, Neuroimaging & Applied Machine Learning

### 🧠 [QAF-Net: Brain Tumor MRI Classification on Edge Devices](https://github.com/isratjahanrani/QAF-Net-Brain-Tumor-MRI-Classification)

**Quantization-Aware Federated Learning with Site-Local Normalization for TinyML Clinical Deployment**

- Investigated brain tumor MRI classification with attention to data privacy, resource-constrained edge devices, and scanner-related domain differences.
- Reported a **55.2% near-duplicate leakage** finding in the evaluated Kaggle benchmark data and developed a decontaminated evaluation protocol.
- Evaluated an INT8-quantized model with a reported size of **606 KB** and **94.7% classification accuracy**.
- Explored quantization and federated learning techniques for resource-constrained medical AI applications.
- **Technologies:** `Python` `TensorFlow/Keras` `TensorFlow Lite (INT8)` `Federated Learning` `OpenCV` `NumPy`

### 🇧🇩 [Hallucination Detection in Pretrained Bangla Generative AI Models](https://github.com/isratjahanrani/Hallucination_in_Pretrained_models)

**Empirical AI Evaluation & Low-Resource Bengali NLP**

- Investigated factual inconsistencies and hallucination patterns in Bengali responses generated by large language models, including GPT, Gemini, and Claude.
- Curated and annotated a benchmark dataset containing **7,043 Bengali question-answer pairs**.
- Reported a **56.5% hallucination rate** within the evaluated dataset and experimental setup.
- Compared BanglaBERT (ELECTRA) and mBERT for hallucination classification.
- Reported **82.47% accuracy and 82.45% Macro F1** for the evaluated model.
- **Technologies:** `Python` `PyTorch` `Hugging Face Transformers` `BanglaBERT` `mBERT` `Data Annotation`

### 🛰️ [AI-Driven CanSat Atmospheric Profiling & Microclimate Monitoring](https://github.com/isratjahanrani/AI-Driven-CanSat-Microclimate-Monitoring)

**Environmental Sensing, Wireless Telemetry & Data Analysis**

- Explored an airborne sensing payload for collecting environmental measurements, atmospheric data, and altitude-related information.
- Integrated environmental sensors, XBee RF communication, and edge anomaly detection.
- Reported a **94%–98% packet delivery ratio (PDR)** in the evaluated telemetry setup.
- Reported **84.7% accuracy** for the evaluated anomaly-detection model.
- **Technologies:** `Embedded C/C++` `XBee RF` `Python` `Scikit-learn` `MQ-135` `BME680`
